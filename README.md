# Immersive Pendor (Beta)

## Description

Immersive Pendor is a solo-built overhaul submod for **Prophesy of Pendor** that deepens the roleplay and quality of life of the base mod. It adds new companion storylines, world-map events, and diplomacy options, and smooths out dozens of everyday rough edges, all while staying true to Pendor's tone and lore.

This is a **beta**: I'm a one-man army bug reports or feedback are hugely appreciated.

See the full version history in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Installation instructions

Install in this exact order, overwriting when prompted:

1. **Prophesy of Pendor v3.9.5** (clean, this exact version)
2. **The Complete Arsenal of Al-Aziz**
3. **Immersive Pendor** (the latest release folder) — always last

Recommended: install it as its own module, so vanilla PoP and your existing saves stay untouched.

1. In your `Mount&Blade Warband\Modules\` folder, copy the whole `Prophesy of Pendor V3.9.5` folder and rename the copy (e.g. `Immersive Pendor 0.45`). The launcher labels each module by its folder name.
2. Install **The Complete Arsenal of Al-Aziz** into that new folder, overwriting when prompted. Don't skip this — the mod needs its items.
3. Copy everything in the Immersive Pendor release folder into that same folder, overwriting when prompted. Keep the folder structure: `Resource\`, `Textures\`, `Sounds\` and `languages\en\` go into the matching subfolders. This also replaces `item_kinds1.txt` and `Resource\ACOK_other.brf`, which is intended.
4. Launch Warband, pick the module, and **start a new game**. Saves from unmodified PoP or from older Immersive Pendor versions may break, because indices changed.

**Don't re-install Al-Aziz after step 3.** It would overwrite Immersive Pendor's items, dialog, menus and scripts. If that happens, copy the Immersive Pendor folder over the top again.

## Main features

- **New companion personal quests** — multi-stage backstory arcs for Sir Ansen, Sir Rayne, Leslie, Diev, Alyssa, and Sir Roland.
- **Random travelling events** — 30 world-map encounters with choices and rewards.
- FREELANCER
- **Expanded diplomacy** — trade agreements between kingdoms, petition your king to declare war or seek peace, return fiefs, and ransom captured kings for peace.
- **Quality-of-life** — troop-upgrade button, higher XP/point gains, more loot, scaling prisoner capacity, faster training & fief improvements, "use your own gear" tournaments with bigger bets/payouts, reduced food drain, compliment/insult lords, feast relation bonuses, and many smaller fixes.
- **Faction-wide troop rebalance** — close to two hundred troop types across every faction (Sarleon, Ravenstern, the Empire, the D'Shar, the Fierdsvain, and Pendor) re-equipped with reworked loadouts, adjusted attributes, weapon proficiencies and skills, and several revised upgrade paths.

## Requirements

- Mount & Blade: Warband
- **Prophesy of Pendor v3.9.5** (required)
- **The Complete Arsenal of Al-Aziz** (required — install it before Immersive Pendor)
- WSE2 recommended (used in development) but not required — runs on standard Warband too.

## Roadmap / Future plans

This is a living project. The rough priorities going forward:

- **Stability first** — squash bugs and polish what's already in the beta. Reported issues take priority over new content.
- **A personal quest for every companion** — extend the multi-stage backstory arcs until *all* of Pendor's companions have one, not just the current handful.
- **A main questline (longer term)** — an overarching story to give the campaign a narrative spine, once the companion arcs are in place.

Plans may shift based on feedback and how much time a one-man army can find. Suggestions are welcome.

# Tweaks 2 — Knighthood Orders (KO)

Source: https://pop3.fandom.com/wiki/Tweaks (Template:Tweaks_2)

---

## Tweak 2a — Order Stronghold garrison
**Files:** `menus.txt`, `conversation.txt`

Enables garrisoning troops in the KO Stronghold and withdrawing them.  
Default garrison: 50 knights, 80 sergeants, 100 Mercenary Armored Crossbowmen, 100 Mercenary Warriors, 20 Snake Cult Followers.

---

## Tweak 2b — Alexios upgrade times
**File:** `conversation.txt`

Default upgrade durations:
- Metal helmets: 50 days (Lordly), 30 days (Reinforced), 20 days (Plain)
- Similar scales for leather, shields, arrows, crossbows, bows, weapons

Alternatives: half-time or one-fifth time versions available.

---

## Tweak 2c — Unique item upgrades via Alexios
**File:** `conversation.txt`

Makes Alexios of Melitine able to upgrade 'unique' items. Items need modifiers added via editor first.

---

## Tweak 2d — Quartermaster horse training
**Files:** `variables.txt`, `quick_strings.txt`, `dialog_states.txt`, `conversation.txt`, `simple_triggers.txt`

Enables training horses to champion status.  
- Training time: `21` days
- Speedup costs: `1450` (spirited), `650` (heavy), `190` (other)

---

## Tweak 2e — Snake Cult Conquest reward
**Files:** `dialog_states.txt`, `conversation.txt`

Gives an honorary reward for conquering the Snake Cult Stronghold.

---

## Tweak 2f — Stronghold rebuild time
**File:** `conversation.txt`

Default rebuild time: `60` days.

---

## Tweak 2g — Guarantee KO chapters at game start
**File:** `scripts.txt`

Guarantees KO chapters spawn at new game start. Requires new game.

---

## Tweak 2h — Increase king chapter creation chance
**File:** `simple_triggers.txt`

Default chance: `15`% for kings to create KO chapters per check.

---

## Tweak 2i — Add KO chapters
**Files:** `scripts.txt`, `menus.txt`, `conversation.txt`

Three methods: before game start, via cheatmenu, or via conversation dialogue.

---

## Tweak 2j — Create chapters in any walled fief
**File:** `conversation.txt`

Allows creating KO chapters in any walled fief of your own kingdom (removes location restriction).

---

## Tweak 2k — Remove chapter creation requirements
**File:** `conversation.txt`

Removes gold, honor, gems, culture, ownership, and relationship prerequisites for creating chapters.

---

## Tweak 2l — Dismantle KO chapters
**File:** `conversation.txt`

Enables dismantling KO chapters in any fief belonging to your faction.

---

## Tweak 2m — Entry troops and prestige costs
**Files:** `scripts.txt`, `conversation.txt`

Change which troops are required for induction and how much prestige it costs.  
Example: Dragons use Ravenstern Knights at 130 prestige cost.

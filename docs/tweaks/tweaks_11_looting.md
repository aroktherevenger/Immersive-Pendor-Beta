## Tweak 11a — Increase loot amount and adjust distribution
**File:** `scripts.txt`

Increases loot received and changes how it is split between the player, companions, and regular troops.

---

## Tweak 11b — Loot items from NPC characters
**File:** `scripts.txt`

Enables looting items directly from defeated NPCs (excludes items flagged as unique).

---

## Tweak 11c — Fix loot order bug (sort by level before processing)
**File:** `scripts.txt`

Fixes an issue where loot depends on the order enemies are defeated — sorts defeated troops by level before processing loot.

# Changelog — Immersive Pendor

Everything Immersive Pendor changes compared to a stock **Prophesy of Pendor v3.9.5** install.

---

## [0.1] — Beta

First public beta. Built on Prophesy of Pendor v3.9.5.

### New companion quests

Multi-stage personal storylines that draw on each companion's backstory:

- **Sir Ansen — "The Falcon":** a young hopeful earns his knighthood through a deed of his own.
- **Sir Rayne — "The Falcon's Right":** restore the lost Order of the Falcon.
- **Leslie — "The Quartermaster's Ledger":** a merchant's past catches up with the company.
- **Diev Wodenssen — "Diev's Reckoning":** hunt the Mystmountain raiders who killed his family.
- **Alyssa — "The Serpent's Shadow":** her old Snake Cult order comes to silence her.
- **Sir Roland — "The Last Blow":** the Paladin of Astraea strikes at the cult that orphaned him.

### World & events

- **Random travelling events:** 30 world-map encounters with meaningful choices and rewards.

### Diplomacy & your kingdom

- Trade agreements between kingdoms.
- Petition your king to declare war on, or seek peace with, a rival realm.
- Return a fief to your king.
- Release a captured enemy king in exchange for peace.
- No honor loss for tolling or robbing caravans of factions you are already at war with.

### Lords, ladies & relations

- Compliment or insult lords in conversation.
- Relation bonus for attending a lord's feast (once per feast).
- Take equipment from captured lords.
- Recruit defeated unique-spawn leaders as companions.
- Give money to poor friendly lords.
- Attack any lord without restrictions.
- Reduced lord escape chance.
- Stronger relation and honor rewards from quests.

### Companions

- Companions no longer leave the party.
- Companion complaints disabled.
- Bodyguard companions stand at your side in scenes.
- Proper equipment restored for mid/high-tier companions.
- Dust of Twilight reliably grants +2 Intelligence.

### Troops & recruitment

- Knighthood Order upgrade system overhaul.
- Custom Knighthood Order (CKO) equipping is cheaper and faster.
- Easier capture of unique spawns.
- Village Blacksmith / School raise the chance of recruiting higher-tier volunteers.
- Sergeants receive half stats from default training.

### Battles & sieges

- Participate in battles while wounded.
- Town militia reinforce villages during bandit attacks.
- A sheriff defends villages and appears there as an NPC.
- No ammunition reduction on sally-outs.
- Fixed the sally-out consciousness bug.

### Tournaments & arena

- Use your own gear in tournaments.
- Added a 5,000-denar bet option.
- Tournament victory pays 5,000 denars.
- Bonus XP per kill in tournaments and arena practice.
- Boosted arena fight rewards.

### Economy & enterprises

- Better enterprise production rates.
- Merchant wealth scales with town prosperity.
- An "Enterprises" report listing all the businesses you own.

### Kingdom & fiefs

- Faster, cheaper fief improvements.
- Send troops to any owned walled fief remotely.
- Garrison sheriff/cleric refill cycle.

### Knighthood orders

- Doubled rank points from all knighthood-order quests.
- Fixed knighthood-order promotion reward tracking.

### Quests

- Deliver Grain / Cattle quests reimburse the item cost.
- Guildmaster auto-offers a new quest when you decline one.
- Looters approach aggressively (fixes the stuck looter quest).
- Brother Randalf reveals all bandit lairs.

### Quality of life

- Higher XP gain rates and more attribute/skill/weapon points per level.
- More loot from battles, and fixed loot ordering.
- Prisoner capacity scales with party size and Prisoner Management.
- Sell garrisoned prisoners to ransom brokers.
- Drastically reduced food consumption.
- Removed luck decay.
- Faster garrison and lord-party training.
- The Inquisition is now hostile to Singalians and outlaws.
- Fixed ambient sounds persisting after leaving taverns and arenas.

---

*This is an early beta — expect rough edges. Bug reports and feedback are hugely appreciated. Start a new game; saves from unmodified PoP may not be compatible.*

# Changelog — Immersive Pendor

Everything Immersive Pendor changes versus a stock **Prophesy of Pendor v3.9.5** install.

Where a change comes from a numbered community PoP tweak, the **(Tweak NN)** reference is given.
Features list **Access:** — how to actually reach the content in-game.

---

## [0.500]

**Start a new game — saves from 0.46 and earlier will not load.** 0.500 replaces the planned 0.46 and is a save break: older saves show "Incomp. Mod. Ver.!" on the Load screen. From now on, hotfixes (0.501, 0.502 …) and minor patches (0.510, 0.520 …) keep your 0.500 saves; only the next big release (0.600) breaks saves again. The version shows as v0.5 in the main-menu corner.

### New systems & content

- **Reworked scenes for the D'Shar lands, the bandit lairs and the Hidden Mines of Al-Aziz.** A scene pack by **FrozenPainter** (used with permission, all credit to FrozenPainter) replaces 22 existing scenes: Yasin is now a nomad yurt camp in the hills; the D'Shar Raider Lair and the Brigand Camp have new layouts; the Snake Cult Shrine and Vanskerry Landing are reworked; and 16 more D'Shar villages get fixed walking paths for the people in them. Some scenes now have saddled horses standing about. **Access:** visit Yasin or any D'Shar village; attack a D'Shar Raider Lair, Brigand Camp, Snake Cult Shrine or Vanskerry Landing on the map.

- **A chest in the Hidden Mines of Al-Aziz.** The mines now have a chest you can open. The first time you go in, it holds 3 Wine. **Access:** Hidden Mines of Al-Aziz on the map → Enter → walk to the chest and press the use key.

- **Red Brotherhood caches reworked.** When a tavern traveller sells you a map to a hidden Red Brotherhood cache, the camp now fits the land it sits on: a landing with crates and beached boats near water, a fenced camp at a cave mouth on the steppe, and the old forest camp inland. Caches no longer appear in snow or desert. The traveller offers one more often (about 1 in 4 maps instead of 1 in 6), and the camp is led by a Brotherhood Guildsman. The chest can now also hold a Gold Bar with Noldor Trade Goods (20%), a Small Pouch of Diamonds (5%) or a Qualis Gem (1%), on every cache, not just the first. On the map it is now called **Brotherhood Cache**. Needs a new game. **Access:** talk to travellers in town taverns; when one offers a map to a Red Brotherhood cache, pay 200 denars, find the Brotherhood Cache near the named village, win the fight and open the chest.

- **Jatu Raider Camp.** The Jatu now keep a raiders' camp out on the Jatu Plains, full of plunder taken from the Noldor. Fight its waves and its Warlord on foot, and the loot includes Noldor gear you can't buy in shops. Clear it and a new camp rises later. **Access:** ask the lord of Poinsbruk or Laria for a task. If he names Jatu Raiders, accept, and the camp shows on your map. Or ride the Jatu Plains until you stumble on it.

- **Chapter 2: the paymaster's camp now matches your strength, and the journal links Sarleon and Marleons.** Sneaking into Sarleon to confront the watch commander now gets its own line. The camp, now called the Marleons Road Camp, gains tougher men if you're level 10 or higher when Chapter 2 starts. **Access:** Main Story Chapter 2, from the merchant's table in your start-town tavern.

- **Marrying a Noldor lady now takes a real trial, and her father gives a dowry.** Iranith and Elasai at Elacrai will only agree once your standing with the Noldor is at least 95, you have won an Elacrai tournament, you carry a Qualis Gem in your own pack, and you are sworn to a kingdom or rule one. Until then the lady tells you exactly what is still missing, and her father checks again when you ask for her hand. He takes the Qualis Gem as the bride gift and lets you choose a dowry of Noldor make: a Masterwork Noldor Runesword, a Superb Noldor Trimmed Ranger Garb or a Masterwork Noldor Composite Bow (if your pack is full, the wedding goes on without it). Builds on the Noldor ladies from 0.45 (Tweak 17y). **Access:** Elacrai → visit Iranith or Elasai → "Do you think that we may have a future together, my lady?" → then ask her father, Sadren (Iranith) or Calanon (Elasai), for her hand.

- **Your wife can ride with you.** Any wife, Noldor wives included, can now join your party as a companion with her own stats and gear. Talk to her from the Party screen to look at her equipment or send her home. If you are defeated she slips away home instead of being taken prisoner (a wife with no home yet stays with you). She can't be your companion and your minister at once. Household storage, feasts and outfit changes stay at home. She brings armour but no weapon, and draws a weekly wage like any hero. See [New Mechanics](NEW_MECHANICS.md#your-wife-can-ride-with-you). **Access:** talk to your wife at home → "Ride with me for a while."; on the road, Party screen → select her → Talk.

- **Hire elite troops from a town's Master-at-Arms.** Seven towns now have a Master-at-Arms by the castle gate who trains the region's famous troops, a few men a week, for good standing with the town and a high price: Valdis Huscarls (Javiksholm), Empire Centurions (Janos), Marleons Heavy Cavalry (Marleons), Iceguard Rangers (Poinsbruk), Singalian Temptresses (Singal), Guardian Empire Knights (Cez) and D'Shar Djaha Archers (Ishkoman). See [New Mechanics](NEW_MECHANICS.md#hire-elite-troops-from-a-master-at-arms). **Access:** enter one of those towns → "Visit the Master-at-Arms." (or walk to the castle gate and talk to him) → "I am looking for fighting men."

- **Decide the fate of a defeated king.** When a kingdom you are at war with falls, a journal quest, "The Fallen Crown of the ...", tells you where its king is. Hold him and choose: execute him (you take his gear; it costs honor and his old lords' favour), exile him (you take his weapon), take his oath as your vassal, or take him into your party. While he is free he gathers loyalists and rides under his old banner, hostile to everyone. Tavern keepers will sell word of him, and at peace you can ransom him from the lord who holds him. If you are crowned and hold a court, his kingdom's claimant is brought to your prison. **Access:** Journal → Quests; Party screen → Prisoners → select him → Talk; your prison guard; a tavern keeper; the lord who holds him.

- **Main story, Chapter 3: Paid in Full.** After Chapter 2, a rider sent by the merchant's relative brings news that the merchant's household was killed, a coin in each mouth. His surviving relative waits in your start town and asks you to find who paid for it. Hunt the two men the watch commander named in Marleons and Laria: hand each to the watch, or kill him and take his purse. Each choice costs something. Then the courier, laid up at a farmstead on the Laria road, and the man who set the fire. Pick one reward at the end; what is offered depends on how you played. **Access:** finish Chapter 2 and wait three days; the rider finds you anywhere on the map. Chapter 3 adds a new trigger, so it needs a new game.

- **Main story, Chapter 4: The Counting House.** A day after Chapter 3, your relative, back at the tavern, tells you where the courier's pages pointed: a walled house on the D'Shar road where the plague dead were counted. A Heretic coven holds its gate and the old graves around it are open. Break the coven, walk into the archive and hear what the keeper has filed there. Hand him to the Inquisition (the D'Shar will not forget it was done on their land), and the next morning you watch him burn at his own gate; or take his silver and choose whether he dies or is left bound (then Hadric, the coven's rider in red, makes you an offer for your prisoners). If you left kin behind in Chapter 3, they catch up with you on the road. Pick one reward at the end; what is offered depends on what you did with the keeper. **Access:** finish Chapter 3, wait a day, then talk to your relative in your start town's tavern; the Counting House appears on the map south of Sarleon on the D'Shar steppe. Continues on your Chapter 3 save.

- **Buy a 120-day truce with a kingdom you are at peace with.** If you rule a kingdom, your minister can send an emissary to seek a truce of a hundred and twenty days, for a gift. The king names the price: it starts at 20,000 denars between equal realms, is higher if his realm is stronger than yours or he dislikes you, lower if you are the stronger or he likes you, and is never below 10,000 or above 100,000. He agrees only if he recognises your realm, you share an enemy, and you hold none of his old lands; otherwise the emissary tells you why. While it runs, that kingdom will not declare war on you. It shows on the Notes page and can be renewed in its last 20 days. Breaking it by war costs honor, as any truce does. Works on your current 0.500 save; do not remove the mod while a truce emissary is travelling. **Access:** talk to your minister → "I wish to dispatch an emissary" → pick a kingdom → "That our two kingdoms should swear a long truce, of a hundred and twenty days." → when the emissary returns, pay the gift.

- **Hold Left Alt to run faster in towns.** Walking across a town, a village or a castle hall to reach the person you came for takes a while. Hold Left Alt while you walk on foot and you move about two-thirds faster; let go and you are back to normal. It does nothing on horseback, in battles, tournaments, the arena or lairs, or while anyone in the scene is fighting you. Idea from Warsword Conquest, suggested by the community. **Access:** hold Left Alt while walking on foot in a town centre, the tavern, a village, a castle hall or your Old Ruins camp.

- **A Noldor husband (Tweak 17z).** A woman can now marry Ilaedanir, a Noldor noble who waits in Elacrai's hall. The terms are the same as for a Noldor wife: standing 95 with the Noldor, an Elacrai tournament win, a Qualis Gem in your pack, and a kingdom you serve or rule. He tells you what is still missing. When you qualify, the gem is taken, you pick one gift from his house (a Noldor runesword, ranger's garb or bow) and he joins your party. After that he works like a Noldor wife: he can ride with you or go home, goes home if you are defeated, can ask Elacrai for troops and can be your minister. He comes without a weapon. **Access:** Elacrai → "Go to the Noldor castle hall." → talk to Ilaedanir → "Would you take me as your wife?"

### Changes
- **Chapter 2 now starts in person, not by letter.** At the end of Chapter 1 the merchant asks you to come back to his tavern table in three days, whatever you answered, and your journal says where. Come back and he tells you what his rescued kin overheard; come early and he tells you it is not time yet. Chapter 3 now opens with one page, a rider sent by the merchant's relative, instead of a cheerful letter from the merchant followed by the news of his death. Needs a new game. **Access:** finish Chapter 1 → three days later, your start town → "Visit the tavern." → talk to the merchant.

- **Story chapters now tell you what comes next.** After Chapters 1, 2 and 3 you hear what comes next, and your journal says what you are waiting for. The next chapter comes on time, 3 days after Chapters 1 and 2 and 1 day after Chapter 3, even if you rest or camp in between. **Access:** finish a story chapter and check your journal.

- **Formations and battle AI v5.** The battle formations and enemy battle AI are upgraded to Motomataru's Formations and AI v5.1. Open-field battles start with your divisions placed around you and holding, enemy and allied lords of disciplined armies fight in formation with v5's tactics, and a new camp menu option holds its settings and a summary of the battle keys (1-9 select a division, F1-F4 give orders, F4 picks Ranks, Shield Wall, Wedge or Square). Bandits and similar foes still fight as a mob. **Access:** automatic in field battles; camp menu > "Formations and AI options." for settings; camp menu > POP options > "Enable Formations" turns it off and gives you the old battle AI. Credit: Formations and AI v5.1 by Motomataru.


- **Your citizen minister can now do everything a companion minister can.** The "prominent citizen from the area" you can appoint when your kingdom is founded now offers political quests, emissaries, indicting a vassal and granting fiefs, so you can keep every companion in your party. You can also switch back to him later from any minister. Works on your current 0.500 save. (Tweak 13b) **Access:** your court → talk to your minister; to switch, talk to your minister → "I wish for you to retire as minister." (or "I wish you to rejoin my party.") → "A prominent citizen from the area."

- **Autoresolve now uses your party's skills.** When you order your troops to attack without you, **Tactics** counts: for each level your party's Tactics is above the enemy's, your side hits 1% harder and takes 1% less, up to 15%. **Surgery** brings back 2% of your killed per level as wounded instead of dead. **Prisoner Management** captures 1% more of the enemy's fallen per level when you win. The enemy's Tactics and Surgery count by the same rules, and battles between AI parties on the map use them too. In an even fight, maxed Tactics against none raises your chance of winning from about half to more than 4 in 5. The Surgery, Tactics and Prisoner Management descriptions on the Character screen now say what they do in autoresolve (English text only). See [New Mechanics](NEW_MECHANICS.md#autoresolve-and-your-partys-skills). **Access:** any battle → "Order your troops to attack without you." (also when joining a battle, and in sieges).

- **Raiding breaks a truce.** Raiding a caravan or village of a kingdom you have a truce with, including the 20-day truce after a war, now ends that truce at once, and they may declare war again. **Access:** as the ruler of your own kingdom, attack a caravan or raid a village of a kingdom you hold a truce with; a red message says the truce is broken.

- **No background starts you at war with a great kingdom.** The minor noble, former knight, wandering nomad and "a letter that changed your life forever" used to start at −10 (at war) with one to three of Sarleon, Ravenstern, D'Shar and the Baccus Empire, which also hid their start towns. Every new character now starts at peace with all five kingdoms and can choose any start town. Penalties with knight orders, the Inquisition, the Snake Cult and the two kings are unchanged. New games only. **Access:** start a new game and pick any background; the "you arrive in Pendor" screen lists all five towns.

- **Six top-tier troops can now upgrade into their faction's elite.** Vanskerry Hersir → Vanskerry Jarl, Mystmountain Warrior → Mystmountain Shaman, Mettenheim Hauptmann → Mettenheim Forlorn Hope, Veccavi Knight → Veccavi Bloodsworn Guard, Barclay Imperial Lancier → Barclay Imperial Chevalier, Jatu Battle Rider → Jatu Warlord. Each used to be the end of its tree. The Mettenheim and Barclay upgrades need a lot of experience (about six times a Princeps becoming a Centurion). Works on your current 0.500 save. Ideas from the "Improved troop trees" submod (Nexus 6654) and a community change set for 0.5. **Access:** party screen → select one of these troops (trained up the mercenary trees, hired, a companion's gift, or freed prisoners) → **Upgrade** once it has the experience.

- **More troops can upgrade.** Bandits now climb one step at a time: Bandit → Brigand → Highwayman → Red Brotherhood Thief → Red Brotherhood Guildsman. Highwaymen can also become Notorious Outlaws, D'Shar Bandits become D'Shar Outlaw Chieftains, Farmers can become Town Militiamen, and Mystmountain Shamans become Bearclaw Berserkers (who fight on foot). The Troop Trees screen shows all of them. **Access:** party screen → select the troop → Upgrade, once it has the XP. Bandit-line troops come from recruited prisoners, Red Brotherhood Thieves from the Red Brotherhood company, Shamans from the Mystmountain mercenary tree or Adonja's gift.

- **Troop routing is now off by default in new games.** Soldiers no longer flee from battle when their morale breaks. Your own order to retreat still works, and a new order still calls them back. **Access:** camp → POP options → Disable troop routing (ticked = no routing). Existing campaigns keep their setting: tick it to turn routing off.

- **Main story places are easy to find.** The Chapter 2 camp and the Chapter 4 coven now show as tents and the Chapter 3 farmstead as houses, and they show on the map from any distance, like a village. The camp, the farmstead and the coven also show their name on the map until you ride into them, and the coven no longer hides behind the Counting House. The Inquisitor's escort in Chapter 4 also shows from any distance. The Chapter 2 quest note links the camp: click its name to see it on the map. Each place leaves the map once you're done with it, and the Counting House now goes away when Chapter 4 ends. Needs a new game. **Access:** Main Story Chapters 2–4; open the quest card's current note and click the place's name.

- **Main story: you meet the people in person, and fight when it comes to that.** In Chapter 2 you wait for the watch commander in the alley behind Sarleon's watch house and fight him and his two hired swords; once he yields, you decide what happens to him in conversation, and he gives up the next two names himself. In Chapter 3 you follow each night gateman into the lanes behind his gatehouse in Marleons and Laria and do the same; the second one tells you about the courier. Lose, fall or step away and he gets away until you try again; knock him out and you can wait for him to come round. Your relative now waits in your start town's tavern for all of Chapter 3, and you talk to the man who set the fire instead of picking from a menu. Needs a new game. **Access:** Chapter 2: Sarleon → "Seek out the watch commander named on the tally." → "Step out and face him." · Chapter 3: Marleons or Laria → "Look for the man the watch commander named." → "Go after him." · your start town → "Visit the tavern." · after the Roadside Farmstead fight → "Talk to him."

- **Main story: tougher fights and better rewards in Chapters 2 to 4.** The story camps keep their numbers but field better men, a step up each chapter: bandits and sellswords at the Chapter 2 camp, bandits at the farmstead with crossbowmen and sellswords from level 10 (mercenary infantry from level 20), and more Heretic Worshipers in the Chapter 4 coven. Each chapter also pays a bit more: renown +10 / +20 / +25 on every path. The justice path now pays a bounty (200 / 800 / 1,000 denars), and the tyranny path pays more gold (500 / 2,800 / 3,200), so it still pays more. Needs a new game. **Access:** play the main story from Chapter 2.

- **Main story: clearer, with names and outcomes that follow your choices.** Each chapter's journal now says what you are trying to find out. The men you hunt in Chapters 2 and 3 have names: Captain Osmund Carr of the Sarleon watch, the gatemen Tobin Ashe and Wat Fennick, and Corran, the man who set the fire. What happens follows what you did. Sneak into Sarleon and expose Carr, and you leave him tied up for the watch to find. Kill a gateman, and his town thinks less of you. The towns no longer thank you for killing their own gatemen; the Chapter 3 reward now comes from the courier's baggage. The Marleons gateman, who has friends in the castle, is the one who walks free if you hand him in. Your journal also shows how the towns talk of you, by the law or by the knife. The farmstead, the courier's fate and the heretics at the Counting House are explained. The end of Chapter 4 says the story continues in a later version. Chapter 2's quest is now called "The Guards' Pay". Needs a new game. **Access:** play the main story from the start-town merchant's Chapter 1 talk; Notes → Quests shows each chapter's journal.

- **Main story: every lead has a reason, and the big moments are seen, not read.** Chapter 2's lead now comes from what the merchant's rescued kin overheard their guards say. Captain Carr shows you his pay, foreign silver with every coin the same, and the same coins turn up in the mouths of the dead in Chapter 3. Both night gatemen tell you a man rode out to the farmstead for his pay, smelling of smoke. You now walk into the street to see the burned merchant's house, and your relative is waiting at the ruin. The Chapter 2 camp and the Chapter 3 farmstead open with a short page saying who these men are, before you attack or leave. Chapter 4 starts in person: your relative asks for a day, then tells you where the Counting House is. If you came for the keeper's silver, you now choose whether he dies or is left bound. The rider in red has a name, Hadric. If you hand the keeper to the Inquisition, the next morning you stand at the Counting House gate and give the word yourself. Only killing Wat Fennick sends his brother after you in Chapter 4. The Inquisitor's escort no longer covers the Counting House's name on the map. Needs a new game. **Access:** play the main story from the start-town merchant's Chapter 1 talk; in Chapter 3 your start town's menu has "Go to what is left of the merchant's house."

- **Snake Cult Armsmen wear lighter armour.** Their Serpent Breastplate over Chainmail drops from 52 to 42 body armour and from 15 to 13 leg armour, which fits a new cult recruit. It also costs 1,900 instead of 4,627. Armour you already own changes too. From a community change set. **Access:** take it as loot from Snake Cult Armsmen, or buy it on the rare occasions a shop has it.

### Fixes

- **The Troop Trees screen now shows every troop that upgrades.** 33 troops appeared on no page before. Three new pages hold them: **Bandits** (looters, bandits and the kingdom deserters), **Minor Factions** (the Vanskerry, Jatu, Mystmountain and Veccavi lines) and **Commoners** (farmers, refugees, militia recruits, the caravan master and the village recruits). Trees already shown on another page are not repeated; click a troop to see what it upgrades into. The upgrade button on one of these troops in the Party screen now opens its page too. **Access:** Reports → Troop trees, pick a page from the list at the top.
- **Companion quest bands no longer hide inside towns.** Sir Roland's cultists near Janos, Alyssa's bands near Janos and Cez, and Diev's raiders near Rane used to spawn right on top of the town, so clicking them selected the town instead. They now wait on open ground just outside the walls. If one is already stuck inside a town in your save, it moves out within a day. **Access:** Sir Roland's, Alyssa's and Diev's personal quests.

- **Captured kings can be asked for peace every time you talk to them.** The "Grant me peace between our kingdoms" offer showed up when you first spoke to a captured enemy king, but disappeared if you talked to anyone else before going back to him. It now always appears while the rules allow it: you are a king, he is a king, your kingdoms are at war, and he hasn't refused you in the last week. **Access:** party screen → your prisoners → talk to a captured enemy king.

- **No more endless loop when following your lord into a siege.** While enlisted, following your commander into a siege battle could send you to "Pass through the siege lines" over and over, with no way out of the menus. You now go straight to your lord's side: the besiegers' camp if he is attacking, or the defenders if he is holding the town. **Access:** enlisted with a lord → "Follow your commander into battle" while he besieges or defends a town or castle.

- **Helmets no longer float in the air after a decapitation.** When a soldier lost his head, many helmets dropped to the ground at head height and hung there in mid-air. Those helmets now simply come off with the head instead of being dropped. Helmets that sit correctly on the ground still drop as before. Tested in battle before release. **Access:** any battle with decapitations switched on (Camp menu, "Battle gore").

- **"Resume travelling" is back in the Reports menu.** Warband can only show 16 options in a menu. The Reports menu had 17, so with cheats on the last one, "Resume travelling", was cut off and you could get stuck there. "View enterprise report" has moved into the location income report, and its Back button returns there. In the Camp menu, "Discharge" and "Desert" for enlisted soldiers now sit above the cheat options, so they can't be cut off either. Tested in game before release. **Access:** Reports → "View location income report" → "View enterprise report"; Camp menu while enlisted.

- **The arena master now quotes the right prize.** He said being the last fighter standing pays 4000 denars, but it actually pays 1000 (it was lowered in an earlier balance pass). He now says 1000. **Access:** any town arena → talk to the arena master → "Is there a reward?" or "Can you tell me about the rewards again?"

- **Red Brotherhood caches by a river no longer get the sea landing.** The landing with beached boats now only appears by the sea or the inland sea. A cache next to a river uses the forest camp instead. **Access:** buy a Red Brotherhood cache map from a tavern traveller and attack the Brotherhood Cache.

- **Red Brotherhood caches beside a river bridge no longer get the sea landing either.** The river fix above missed caches whose nearest water was a bridge over a river: they still got the landing with beached boats. Now only caches by the sea or the inland sea get it. A cache that already appeared before this fix keeps its scene until you clear it. **Access:** buy a Red Brotherhood cache map from a tavern traveller and attack the Brotherhood Cache.

- **Clearing a bandit lair always gives loot, and the lair goes away.** Lair loot was scaled by your share of your last field battle. If you had never fought one, that share was zero: you got no loot, and the cleared lair stayed on the map. For a Brotherhood Cache, that also stopped travellers offering you new cache maps. Lair loot now counts you as fighting alone, which you are. This also means lairs no longer give less loot because your last battle was fought beside a large allied army. Applies to every bandit lair. **Access:** attack and clear any bandit lair or Brotherhood Cache.

- **Main Story Chapter 2's last journal note no longer looks like a task.** The closing note named Marleons and Laria in a way that read like orders to go there, but there is nothing to do there yet. It now says the watch commander named two other men in those towns, and that there is nothing more to be done about them for now. **Access:** quest journal, after finishing "The Crooked Captain".

- **The watch commander's last words in Chapter 2 now match your choice.** All three choices used to end with the same text, which said he talked "without being pressed" even when you refused his money to make him talk. Now he answers each choice in his own words, and names the two men in Marleons and Laria whichever you pick. Rewards are unchanged. **Access:** Main Story Chapter 2, in the alley behind Sarleon's watch house, once he yields.

- **Bandit-lair quests pay their gold reward once.** When you reported a cleared lair, the lord paid the reward twice. He now pays what he offered. **Access:** report a cleared lair to the lord who gave you the quest.

- **Morgh's Editor can edit troops again.** Opening any troop in Morgh's Editor showed "Error # 9 in ShowTroop", so players who edit the mod could not change troops. Fixed; nothing changes in the game itself. **Access:** open the mod's troops in Morgh's Editor (Troop editor).

- **Ransoming a captive lady now pays.** When you took a fief and told a lady held there to "contact your family to arrange for a ransom", you lost honor and her goodwill, but her family never made an offer and she waited forever. Her family now sends an offer like they do for captured lords, and you can accept or reject it. If you change your mind and let her go, in the conversation or from the party screen, she is released properly. **Access:** capture a castle or town with a lady inside → talk to her → "You assume wrong, my lady!" → "Contact your family to arrange for a ransom, my lady." → wait for the offer.

- **Trade agreements you negotiate are now actually signed.** When your emissary came back with a yes to a trade agreement, the message read "Trade agreement signed: Kingdom of Pendor and No Faction." and no agreement was made with any kingdom. It now names the kingdom you chose, and that kingdom's report lists the agreement. If this happened to you, you can propose the agreement again. **Access:** as ruler of your own kingdom, Camp → talk to your minister → propose a diplomatic initiative → choose a kingdom that has recognised you → "That we propose a trade agreement with him."

- **Diev's Reckoning now leaves the quest list.** After you told Diev the raid band was dead and chose his reward, the quest stayed in your journal saying "Talk to Diev Wodenssen to claim your reward". It now closes as soon as you choose. If it is already stuck in your save, it clears within a day. **Access:** Diev's personal quest → Party screen → talk to Diev after the raid band is destroyed.

- **Finished main story chapters now leave the quest list.** When Chapter 2, 3 or 4 ended, its card stayed among your active quests, marked as won. It now closes when you take the chapter's reward, and counts as a completed quest. If a card is already stuck on your save, it clears within an in-game hour. **Access:** finish a main story chapter by taking its reward, then open Notes.

- **Ranged troops carry ammo that fits their weapon.** Heroine Adventurers are now always archers with Khergit arrows, like the Hero Adventurer (some used to turn up with a crossbow and cheap arrows). Maiden Adventurers carry steel bolts. Bounty Hunters have their crossbow back, so their bolts are no longer wasted. Empire Deserters no longer carry bolts they cannot shoot. The village hunter carries arrows for his bow. **Access:** any battle with these troops, on your side or the enemy's.

- **Empire Centurions throw as well as they fight.** Their throwing skill was 300 against 380 in melee, so they threw their spears worse than the Princeps they are trained from. It is now 380. **Access:** party screen → an Empire Centurion → his weapon skills.

- **Marriage texts no longer assume a wife.** The Qualis Gem is now a "marriage gift" and the gift message says "Gift received". The minister menu says "Appoint your spouse", the feast notice says "Your spouse has started preparations", and after a defeat your spouse "is making for home". **Access:** marry a Noldor spouse, then found a kingdom, host a feast or lose a battle with them in your party.

- **The stronghold blacksmith upgrades pistols properly.** Alexios quoted 0 days for a pistol and then kept it. Pistols now take 10–25 days like crossbows and muskets, and a masterwork pistol is no longer offered. A pistol he kept before this fix is returned the next time you talk to him. **Access:** any Knight Order stronghold → talk to the blacksmith.

- **Six reworked armours now count at their real values everywhere.** The Black Brigandine & Plate (with and without cape), Dark Strapped Mail Coat, Dark Chain Coat, White & Black Platemail and Black Mail & Plate were tweaked in earlier versions, but companions picking up loot, the Order Blacksmith's prices and knightly gear still used their old armour and price. They now use the values you see on the item. New games only. **Access:** automatic in a new game.

## [0.44]

### New: the Main Story begins (Chapters 1 and 2)

Immersive Pendor now has a main story. It starts from the merchant whose relative you rescue at the very beginning of every campaign, and it will be released one chapter at a time so we can hear how each one plays before writing the next. This release has the first two chapters.

- **Chapter 1, "The Merchant's Debt."** The merchant quests that open every campaign are now the first chapter of the story, retitled *Five Men*, *The Hostage* and *The Lair*. They play exactly as before. What changes is the ending: instead of thanking you and wishing you luck, the merchant asks why the band that took his relative stayed camped near town instead of carrying their captive off, as their kind always do. You can tell him it is none of your business, tell him they were slow and stupid, or agree that somebody paid them to wait. **Access:** start a new campaign and finish the merchant's quests; the new conversation comes when you hand his relative back.

- **Chapter 2, "The Crooked Captain."** A few days later a courier brings a letter. The merchant has been back through his books, and part of the ransom money went to one man, month after month, since before his relative was ever taken. That man keeps a camp north of Sarleon on the road to Marleons. Break the camp and you find a tally roll that names a watch commander of Sarleon. Corner him in Sarleon and decide what happens to him: have him stripped of his post (honour), take his money and let it lie (150 denars), or refuse the bribe and make him talk, which leaves him at his post. Your choice is remembered for a later chapter. The whole chapter is tracked in your journal as *The Crooked Captain*. **Access:** the letter arrives on its own about three days after Chapter 1. **No new game is needed.** If you already finished the merchant's quests on an existing save, or turned the merchant down, the letter still finds you, about three days after you load in. Once the camp is gone, enter Sarleon and choose *"Seek out the watch commander named on the tally."* from the town menu. It also works if you have sneaked into the town.

**Tell us how it plays.** Is it clear where to go and what to do? Does the pacing between the two chapters feel right? That decides how Chapter 3 gets written, and it is the one thing we cannot test ourselves.

It contains spoilers. **Access:** the link above, or the file in the download.

## [0.35]

## [0.34]

Greedy or dishonourable choices carry an honor cost; charity, duty, and respect for the fallen are what earn honor.

## [0.32]

### New systems & content

The number of battle plans you can keep grows with your **Tactics** skill: 1 plan at Tactics 2, 2 at Tactics 5, 3 at Tactics 10. *Access:* Camp → "Plan your battle formations" to build your plans; then pick one from the menu that appears when you charge the enemy.

Earns you standing and favor with the knight's Order, a little honor, and experience. Each Order will only ask again after some time has passed. *Access:* talk to a knighthood-order knight in any town tavern and choose "Is there a rival Order you'd have me drive off?"

## [0.3]

### New systems & content

- **Arena practice fights are now once per day.** You can enter the arena training melee a single time per in-game day; after your bout the Arena Master tells you to rest and return on the morrow, and the option reopens at the next day's dawn. Keeps arena training from being an endless same-day XP/gold grind. Tournaments are unaffected. *Access:* talk to the Arena Master in any town and choose to join the melee.

### Changes

- **Random travelling events no longer spoil their outcomes.** Event choices used to list their rewards right in the option text (e.g. "(+3 renown, Sarleon +1)"). Those hints are gone — you now decide blind and live with the consequences.
- **Rewards are revealed afterward in the message log.** Once you make a choice, what you gained shows in the top-left log — now including gold and items, which previously arrived silently.
- **Companion-quest rewards are spelled out in the log too.** Finishing a companion's personal quest now reports the experience gained, any item received, and a note that the companion has grown more skilled — rewards that used to be applied silently.
- **Honor and faction-standing changes now show the amount.** Messages used to just say "You gain honor." or "{Faction} relation increased." — now they read "You gain 2 honor." and "Kingdom of Sarleon thinks better of you (+1)", so every reward states exactly how much (this applies game-wide, not just to the new content).
- **Travelling events resolve cleanly on the spot.** Options that sounded like they sent you off on an untracked errand (escort the merchant, take the girl to town, send the prisoner to the Inquisition) are reworded so the outcome clearly happens then and there — no more "we'll do X" with nothing to follow up on.

# Companion Quest Guide

> **Spoiler warning:** this page lists every step, choice and reward of the Immersive Pendor companion quests.

Six companions have a personal questline: **Ansen**, **Sir Rayne**, **Leslie**, **Diev Wodenssen**, **Alyssa** and **Sir Roland**. Every companion can also be sent to the Noldor for training (see the last section).

## How these quests work

- **Talking to a companion** means: open the party screen, select the companion, and click **Talk**. New quest options appear in the companion's normal conversation menu.
- **The 30 day clock** starts the first time the companion is in your party. Dismissing them does not reset it. The clock is checked once per in-game hour.
- **"Something on their mind" notice.** When a quest can be started, a message appears once: *"(Name) seems to have something weighing on their mind. You may wish to speak with them."* It only appears while the companion is in your party, and only once per companion.
- **Reward messages.** When a companion gains skills from their quest, a message names each one, for example *"Alyssa: Power Strike +1."*
- **Honor and relation numbers.** Immersive Pendor multiplies every honor and relation gain by 1.5 (rounded down). The numbers below are what you actually receive and see on screen.
- **Quest bands** you must defeat are set to hold their position and are not attacked by other parties, so only you can destroy them. Many of them look like ordinary bands of the same type.
- **Timing.** Some steps are checked once per in-game day, so a quest band can take up to a day to appear, and the next step can take up to a day to unlock after you win.
- **Old saves.** These quests rely on background checks that were added in specific versions. A save started on a version without them may not unlock a quest (not confirmed for every version). A new campaign is the safe choice.

## Ansen: The Falcon's First Squire

Ansen wants to become a knight. The only order that might take him is the outlawed Order of the Falcon, whose last willing master is **Sir Rayne**. You need both companions for this quest.

### How to start
- Ansen has been in your party for **30 days**.
- Your renown is **100 or more**.
- Talk to Ansen: **"You look troubled, Ansen."**

### Stage 1: the confession (a permanent choice)
| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| "Frederick does not decide who earns spurs in this company. I do. We start in the morning." | The quest starts. |
| "Half this company is still walking because of you, Ansen. Keep the tent. That is my answer, and it will not change." | **Ends the questline for good.** Ansen gets **First Aid +1, Surgery +1, Wound Treatment +1** (a message lists them). |

### Stage 2: Sir Rayne's sponsorship
- Recruit Sir Rayne if you have not. With Ansen in your party, talk to Sir Rayne: **"My companion Ansen wishes to take the spurs. Would you stand for him?"**
- Rayne refuses at first. Answer **"He has earned the right to be heard. That much you owe him."** to win him over. Answering "You have the right of it. I will let it lie." just ends the talk; you can ask again later.
- When Rayne agrees, a band called the **Snowcat Raiders** appears near **Rane** within an hour. A message tells you they have been sighted.

### Stage 3: break the Snowcat Raiders
- Find the Snowcat Raiders near Rane and destroy them. The quest advances within an hour.
- Ansen does not have to be in your party for the win to count, though Sir Rayne asks for it in his speech.

### Stage 4: the vigil (choices decide Ansen's reward)
With Sir Rayne in your party, talk to Ansen: **"It is time, Ansen. Let us hold the vigil. Sir Rayne is ready."** Ansen describes three trials. Each answer adds points to one of five virtues:

| Trial | Answer | Virtue |
|---|---|---|
| 1. A drunken squire mocks Ansen | "Stand and face him." | Courage +2 |
| | "Stay kneeling. Pray louder." | Faith +2 |
| | "Speak to him without rising." | Honor +2 |
| 2. A wounded villager falls in the aisle | "Break the vigil. Treat him." | Mercy +2 |
| | "Refuse. Let the servants tend him." | Honor +1 |
| | "Pray for him. Do not move from your place." | Faith +1 |
| 3. A woman who once refused him | "Look back at her. Hold her gaze." | Courage +1 |
| | "Lower your eyes. She was right to doubt you." | Humility +2 |
| | "Pity her. She has lost more than she knows." | Mercy +1 |

The virtue with the most points decides Ansen's skill package. Ties go to the first virtue in this order: Humility, Courage, Honor, Mercy, Faith.

| Dominant virtue | Ansen gains |
|---|---|
| Courage | Power Strike +1, Ironflesh +1, Riding +1 |
| Honor | Leadership +1, Tactics +1, Trainer +1 |
| Mercy or Faith | Wound Treatment +1, Surgery +1, First Aid +1 |
| Humility | Power Strike +1, Leadership +1, First Aid +1 |

In practice:
- **Humility:** answer "Lower your eyes." in trial 3. This gives Humility unless you also picked "Stay kneeling." and "Pray for him." (healer package) or "Speak to him without rising." and "Refuse." (Honor package).
- **Otherwise your trial 1 answer picks the package:** "Stand and face him." gives Courage, "Speak to him without rising." gives Honor, "Stay kneeling." gives the healer package.
- "Break the vigil. Treat him." plus "Pity her." gives Mercy 3 and turns a Courage or Honor vigil into the healer package.

### Stage 5: the dubbing
With Ansen in your party, talk to Sir Rayne: **"He has stood your vigil."** Rayne knights him.

**Rewards**
- Ansen is renamed **Sir Ansen** and gains the skill package from the vigil.
- You receive the **Falcon Plate** (body armour; equip it on Ansen yourself if you like).
- You gain **1000 experience**.
- The quest is completed.

### Afterwards (flavour only)
With Sir Ansen in your party, one extra conversation unlocks with each of **Frederick of Mettenheim**, **Adonja** and **Boadice**. If Sir Rayne's own quest (below) is also finished, Sir Ansen has one more line about the order. None of these give rewards.

### Ways to lock yourself out
- Choosing the "Keep the tent" answer in stage 1 ends the questline permanently.
- Without Sir Rayne the quest cannot get past stage 1.

## Sir Rayne: The Falcon's Right

Sir Rayne wants to win back the standing of the Order of the Falcon, which still holds a charter under the old Validus Charta.

### How to start
- Sir Rayne has been in your party for **30 days**.
- Your renown is **150 or more**.
- Talk to Sir Rayne: **"Sir Rayne, you carry something. Speak your mind."**

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| "Properly it is, then. Where does a chartered order begin?" | The quest starts. |
| "The Falcon died with its honour intact, Sir Rayne. Leave it there." | Relation with Sir Rayne **+3**. **The questline ends for good**; the offer does not come back. |

### Stage 1: win a tournament
- Win a tournament in **any town** after accepting. The quest advances within an hour.

### Stage 2: Larissa in Rane
- Go to **Rane**, enter the tavern and talk to **Larissa**, the tavern keeper. Sir Rayne does not need to be with you.
- All three answers work the same way:
  - "Mistress, the charter you remember is the one we mean to restore..." (only shown if your own Persuasion is 5 or more)
  - "Ask at the tournament grounds. The Falcon has been winning under its own name again."
  - "Sir Rayne sent me. He has staked his name on it, and he does not spend that lightly."

### Stage 3: the refounding (a permanent choice)
Talk to Sir Rayne: **"Sir Rayne. It is time."** Choose the code of the restored order:

| Oath | Sir Rayne gains |
|---|---|
| "Strict and lawful. Exactly as the Charta sets it down." | Leadership +1, Trainer +1, Persuasion +1 |
| "Open to anyone worth the name. Birth does not enter into it." | Trainer +2, First Aid +1 |
| "Proven by deeds. The charter travels at the point of a sword or it does not travel." | Power Strike +1, Ironflesh +1, Riding +1 |

**Rewards (any oath)**
- Sir Rayne's skill package above.
- You receive the **Order of the Falcon Kite Shield**.
- You gain **1000 experience**.
- The quest is completed.

### Ways to lock yourself out
- Declining Sir Rayne's first offer ends the questline permanently.
- After that, every step has an option that is always available.

## Leslie: The Quartermaster's Ledger

Leslie lost a caravan and her kin to raiders on the road from Singal to Janos. She kept the manifest and wants to find the slaver's ledger that holds the rest of the names. This quest costs money and gives no stat rewards.

### How to start
- Leslie has been in your party for **30 days**.
- Your renown is **50 or more**.
- Your party's **Trade skill is 6 or more** (the best Trade among you and your party heroes counts).
- Talk to Leslie: **"You look far from home, Leslie."**
- Accept with "We will find him, Leslie." Choosing "Another time, Leslie." changes nothing, and you can ask again at any time.

### Stage 1: ask a tavern keeper (2000 denars)
- With Leslie in your party and at least **2000 denars**, talk to the tavern keeper in **any town**: "Have you heard word of a slaver's ledger?..."
- Pay with "Two thousand. Send your word." (**-2000 denars**). "Not today, keeper." backs out.

### Stage 2: the factor in Singal
- With Leslie in your party, go to **Singal** and talk to **Morris**, the tavern keeper: "The Singalian factor. Have you a word from him?"
- Choose how to get the ledger:

| Choice | Needs | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| "Three thousand for the book. Done." | 3000 denars | -3000 denars |
| "Leslie reads his book before he can shut it... One thousand, and we take the ledger." | 1000 denars and your own Trade skill 4 or more | -1000 denars |
| "Then I will take the book off him another way." | nothing | free; a band appears near Singal, but fighting it is optional |

All three get you the ledger right away. The 1000 denar option checks your own Trade skill (not Leslie's or the party's). The band from the third option is a Singalian Slavers band.

### Stage 3: the reckoning (a permanent choice)
Talk to Leslie: **"What does the book tell us, Leslie?"** Three captives from her caravan are still alive.

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| "We go and get them. All three, or we do not come back." | Honor **+1**, relation with Leslie **+3**. A Singalian Slavers band appears near Singal; fighting it is optional and the quest ends either way. |
| "Five thousand denars. Put your road back together, Leslie..." (only shown with 5000 denars) | **-5000 denars**. Afterwards you receive **300 denars every 14 days** while Leslie is in your party, until a total of **7500** has been paid (25 payments, a profit of 2500). A message announces each payment. |
| "Close the book, Leslie. Some things do not get paid back." | Relation with Leslie **+1**. |

The quest is completed with any choice.

**Total cost:** 2000 denars, plus 0, 1000 or 3000 at Singal, plus 5000 if you choose to fund her caravan (which pays back 7500 over time).

### Ways to lock yourself out
None found. If Leslie leaves your party, the next step waits until you take her back.

## Diev Wodenssen: Diev's Reckoning

Diev wants to destroy the Mystmountain band that killed his birth parents. He was raised in Rane by a caravan master who is now dying.

### How to start
- Diev has been in your party for **30 days**. There is no renown requirement.
- Talk to Diev: **"You seem far away, Diev."**

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| "I will stand with you, Diev. Where do we start?" | The quest starts. |
| "Not today, Diev. It will keep, and so will I." | The offer comes back after another **30 days**. |

### Stage 1: ask Larissa in Rane
The band does **not** appear until you have done this step (the journal says so too):
- With Diev in your party, go to **Rane**, enter the tavern and talk to **Larissa**, the tavern keeper: "Mistress, a word. I seek news of a Mystmountain raiding band north of here..."
- Any answer works:
  - "Then tell me what a woman in your trade hears first..." (only shown if your own Persuasion is 5 or more)
  - "For your trouble, mistress. I am paying for the whole of it." (**-200 denars**)
  - "I carry Diev Wodenssen's word. He said you would talk to a friend of his." (free)

### Stage 2: destroy the band
- Within a day, a **Mystmountain Raiders** band appears near **Rane**. It looks like any other Mystmountain band but holds its position. Destroy it.
- The next step unlocks up to a day after the fight.

### Stage 3: the resolution (a permanent choice)
Talk to Diev: **"Diev. It is done. What would you have us do now?"**

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| "We ride to Rane. Your father hears it from you, and we say the word over your mother tonight." | Honor **+3**, relation with Diev **+7**, party morale **+2**, Diev **Power Draw +1** (a message names the skill). |
| "The coin goes to Rane. Your father first, then the other houses that lost what you lost..." | Honor **+1**, relation with Diev **+4**. |

The quest is completed with either choice.

### Ways to lock yourself out
None found.

## Alyssa: The Serpent's Shadow

Alyssa was raised as a priestess of the Snake Cult in a temple near Cez. The cult has learned she is alive and sends a death cult band after her.

### How to start
- Alyssa has been in your party for **30 days**. There is no renown requirement.
- Talk to Alyssa: **"Something hunts you, Alyssa. I see it in how you watch the road."**

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| "Then they have found the both of us. Where do we start?" | The quest starts. |
| "Not today. Stay near the column, and we will talk when there is less on the fire." | The offer comes back after another **30 days**. |

### Stage 1: the hunters near Janos
- Within a day, an **Azi Dahaka Death Cult Marauders** band appears near **Janos**. Destroy it. The next step unlocks up to a day later.

### Stage 2: the shrine near Cez
- Talk to Alyssa: **"The hunters are dead, Alyssa. You can breathe now."** Answer "We will ride. Bring the spear anyway."
- Within a day, a **Snake Cult Shrine** appears near **Cez**. It is a hideout and may only become visible when you get close. Attack and clear it (how the fight plays out is not confirmed). The next step unlocks up to a day later.

### Stage 3: the resolution
Talk to Alyssa: **"The shrine is silent now, Alyssa."** Answer "Keep the armour. Let us go and find your market."

**Rewards**
- Alyssa: **Power Strike +1**.
- You receive the **Anaconda Plate Armor**.
- Honor **+3**, relation with Alyssa **+7**.
- You gain **1000 experience**.
- The quest is completed.

### Ways to lock yourself out
None found.

## Sir Roland: The Last Blow

Sir Roland, a paladin of Astraea, was orphaned when a Snake Cult band burned his village. That band still raids the villages near Janos.

### How to start
- Sir Roland has been in your party for **30 days**. There is no renown requirement.
- Talk to Sir Roland: **"You carry something heavier than your armour, Sir Roland."**

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| "Then we answer it. Lead me to them, Sir Roland." | The quest starts. |
| "Not yet, Sir Roland. They will still be there, and so will your goddess." | The offer comes back after another **30 days**. |

### Stage 1: the raiders near Janos
- Within a day, a **Snake Cult Party** appears near **Janos**. Destroy it. The next step unlocks up to a day later.

### Stage 2: the priestess
- Talk to Sir Roland: **"The raiders near Janos are scattered, Sir Roland. It is done."** Answer "We will run her down, Sir Roland. All of it, not half."
- Within a day, an **Azi Dahaka Death Cult Marauders** band appears near **Janos**. Destroy it. The next step unlocks up to a day later.

### Stage 3: the rite
Talk to Sir Roland: **"The priestess is dead, Sir Roland. It is over."** Answer "Your arm is enough, Sir Roland. Ride with me."

**Rewards**
- Sir Roland: **Power Strike +1**. (He has taken a vow of poverty, so there is no item.)
- Honor **+3**, relation with Sir Roland **+7**.
- You gain **1000 experience**.
- The quest is completed.

### Ways to lock yourself out
None found.

## Noldor training (all companions)

Any of the 20 companions can be sent to the Noldor to train, paid for with a pouch of diamonds.

### Requirements
- Your relation with the **Noldor** is **15 or more**.
- You carry the pouch you want to spend.
- The companion is not already training and is not resting from a previous training.

### How to send a companion
Talk to the companion, choose **"I'd like to ask you something."**, then **"I'd like to send you to the Noldor for training..."**. Each pouch size has its own option. The option text shows how many full trainings of that size this companion has already used.

The pouch is used up and the companion leaves your party. They come back on their own when the training is over. The reward is given when they are back in your party (checked every hour), together with a message.

| Pouch | Time away | Experience | Every weapon proficiency | Rest after return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small Pouch of Diamonds | 10 days | +15,000 | +20 | 10 days |
| Medium Pouch of Diamonds | 14 days | +65,000 | +65 | 14 days |
| Large Pouch of Diamonds | 18 days | +95,000 | +125 | 18 days |

- "Every weapon proficiency" means all seven: one handed, two handed, polearms, archery, crossbows, throwing and firearms. This raise is not limited by the Weapon Master skill.
- **Diminishing returns:** each pouch size gives full gains **3 times per companion**. From the 4th training of the same size, experience and proficiency are halved (Small 7,500 / +10, Medium 32,500 / +32, Large 47,500 / +62). Each size is counted separately.
- **Rest:** after coming back, the companion must rest before training again (any pouch size). Ask "Are you ready to train with the Noldor again?" to see how many days are left.
- No attribute points are given.
